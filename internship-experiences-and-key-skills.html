Professional Experiences and Key Skills 

C. Rodríguez Morales November 6, 2026

Tesla | Technical Project Management Intern

Battery and Drive Unit · Austin, TX · January to July 2024

My first internship taught me how large engineering projects stay on track. I coordinated across engineering, operations, finance, suppliers, and contractors, keeping scope, requirements, milestones, and risks aligned. I also used root-cause analysis to improve how issues were tracked and escalated, so engineering teams could respond faster.

Tools: Microsoft Project, Primavera P6, Bluebeam, Oracle Aconex, Revizto, AutoCad, BIM360, Azure DevOps, Jira

Skills: Project scheduling, stakeholder coordination, risk and issue tracking, root-cause analysis, supplier and procurement coordination

Rivian | Industrial Engineering Intern

Body Shop · Normal, IL · May to August 2025

At Rivian, I moved onto the production floor. I led a Kaizen project, using work measurement and workflow analysis to find and remove wasted motion and rebalance the process. I designed and ran production line trials to test changes before rolling them out, improved material presentation and tooling placement, and analyzed production scenarios to support data-driven decisions.

Tools: MODAPTS, Proplanner, Flexsim

Skills: Kaizen and continuous improvement, line balancing, cycle-time analysis, production trials, work measurement, data analysis

Lucid Motors | Advanced Manufacturing Engineering Intern

Vehicle Engineering · Casa Grande, AZ · June to August 2026

At Lucid, I worked on new product introduction, developing manufacturing processes for new vehicle components: assembly sequence, workstation allocation, cycle time, tooling, and scalability. I led design-for-manufacturing reviews with design, manufacturing, launch, production, and logistics teams, worked with an equipment supplier to translate requirements into equipment design, and wrote and validated work instructions during a pre-production build. I also supported make-vs-buy analysis and tooling and fastener commonization.

Tools: CATIA, Proplanner, AutoCad

Skills: New product introduction (NPI), process development and validation, design for manufacturing (DFM), work instructions, supplier collaboration, tooling requirements

My toolkit

Along with the tools above, I work with Jira, Azure DevOps, Oracle Aconex, ProPlanner, AutoCAD, FlexSim, Minitab, Power BI, Excel VBA, Python, and SQL, and I hold a Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt.

What I took away

Across three very different roles, the same lessons kept coming back:

  • Go see the process. The best improvements came from watching how work actually happens on the floor.
  • Prove it with data. Testing a change before rolling it out builds trust and avoids costly surprises.
  • Manufacturing is a team sport. Every process I worked on depended on aligning design, production, quality, logistics, and suppliers.
